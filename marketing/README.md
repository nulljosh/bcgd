# Marketing

Instagram and Google marketing for Best Choice Garage Doors, run by Joshua for his dad. Not part of the app or the site.

Business: Langley BC, (604) 240-0180, open 24/7, three generations, 30+ years, own team, "we fix it first, no upsell". Instagram is @bcgaragedoorsofficial.

## The plan
Free sample batch first: 10 posts and 2-3 short video ads. Dad keeps his current ads (Google Ads, Door Domination) for 1-2 months. Whatever doesn't bring calls gets cut. Instagram ads aim at calls, with a Call now button. Price is open.

## Rules
Never invent reviews, prices or promos. AI images are never passed off as Dad's real jobs. Real photos beat AI ones.

## What's here
`prompts.md` holds every prompt from the planning chat. `ads-v4.html` is the ad sheet. `ads/` holds the four finished 1080x1350 ads pulled from it: same day, cable safety, the van, repair first.
