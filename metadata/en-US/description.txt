What's on the truck, and what's booked this week. Two questions, and most field-service platforms make you sit through a login and a sync before answering either one.

Doorstock answers them on the drive over.

INVENTORY
Springs, rollers, hinges, openers, weather seal — tracked by category with quick quantity controls. Put a low-stock threshold on any part and everything due for reorder collects itself into one list. Search and filter once the parts list outgrows a single screen.

JOBS
Lead, Quote, Scheduled, Complete, Paid. Add a customer and an address, move a job forward with one tap, swipe away what's finished.

THE MORNING CHECK
Stock count, low-stock warnings and open job totals sit on one screen, so looking over the day takes about ten seconds.

Doorstock ships with a starter catalog of industry-standard part types, so day one isn't an empty list staring back at you. Edit it, add to it, or clear it out and build the catalog your own way.

Your parts list and your customers' names live on your own phone and go nowhere else. Nothing syncs, nothing uploads, and nothing here is tied to a subscription that could lapse and lock you out of your own stock list.
